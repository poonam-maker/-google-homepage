Book your dog's free first visit

Pick a day and time that works for you. The visit starts with a 20 to 30 minute meet and greet, then your pup enjoys their first day of play on us.

1. Choose a date and time

Visits can be booked from tomorrow up to 30 days ahead.

2. Tell us about your pup

This helps us prepare the right playroom and keeps every dog safe.

Before you arrive

Quick checklist for visit day

  • Leash and collar or harness for the parking lot and lobby.
  • Vaccination records for rabies, DHPP and Bordetella, or your vet's contact details and we can request them for you.
  • About 30 minutes for the meet and greet before play begins.
  • Questions welcome, and you can watch the playroom webcams while you're here.
