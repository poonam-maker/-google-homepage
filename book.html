Book your dog's free first visit

Pick a day and time that works for you. The visit starts with a 20 to 30 minute meet and greet, then your pup enjoys their first day of play on us.

What to expect

Your dog's free meet & greet

Before joining daycare, every dog comes in for a complimentary 20–30 minute meet and greet. It's relaxed, fun, and gives us a chance to make sure daycare is a great fit for your pup.

👋

Arrival & intro

You and your dog are greeted by one of our trained team members. We chat briefly about your dog's personality, history and any special needs.

🐾

Supervised intro to play

Your dog enters the playroom in a calm, controlled way and is introduced to a small group. Our staff watches closely to assess comfort level, social cues and energy.

✅

Feedback & next steps

After the intro, we share our observations with you. If it's a great match, your dog stays for their first full day of play — on us. No charge, no commitment.

📋

What to bring

Your dog on a leash and collar or harness. Vaccination records for Rabies, DHPP and Bordetella (or your vet's contact info — we can request them). Allow about 30 minutes for the full meet and greet before play begins.
