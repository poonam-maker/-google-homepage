Overnight and extended stays

Boarding that feels like a sleepover, not a kennel

Travel with peace of mind. Your dog spends boarding days exactly like daycare days, full of supervised play and friends, then rests in calm, home-style spaces overnight.

New here? Start with a free daycare day Members: book boarding
Every boarding day includes

Play hard, rest easy

  • 8 to 10 hours of supervised play and exercise with friends their own size.
  • Enrichment games and mental stimulation throughout the day.
  • Calm nap times in cozy, home-style rest spaces.
  • Individualized care for meals, rest routines and medications.
Why daycare comes first

One free day makes boarding better

We recommend at least one daycare visit, ideally a few, before a first overnight stay. Your dog learns the space, the routine and our team, discovers that this is a place they go home from, and settles into boarding feeling confident instead of confused.

It's also how we get to know their personality, so their boarding stay can be tailored from night one. The first daycare day is free, so there's nothing to lose by starting there.

Packing list

What to bring for a boarding stay

  • Their regular food, portioned if possible, to keep their diet consistent.
  • Medications with clear written instructions.
  • Optional bedding or comfort items. We can't guarantee return condition, so choose items you don't mind getting well loved.
  • Leave toys at home. Personal toys aren't permitted in shared playrooms for everyone's safety.
After their stay

Expect a happy, sleepy pup

Boarding days are full ones. Most dogs come home content and tired from all the play and socialization. Give them quiet time to rest and ease back into their home routine over the first day or two.
