Jumping? Chewing? Nipping?

It's not too late to work on it. In this small group class, you and your dog learn together, with guidance shaped around the behaviours you're dealing with right now.

What you'll work on

Built around your dog's behaviour

🐕

Supervised time around other dogs

Your dog meets other dogs in a calm, structured setting with a trainer watching every interaction.

👁️

How your dog communicates

Learn to read what your dog is telling you — and what other dogs are telling them.

✋

When to step in

Understand when to correct, redirect or simply let it go — and what each situation calls for.

🎯

Jumping, chewing, nipping and more

Your trainer works on the specific behaviours in the room each week — not a fixed curriculum.

Good to know

Before you join

Dogs 1 year + with up-to-date vaccinations

Please bring proof of vaccinations when you register. Dogs must be at least 1 year old to join.

Arrive on time

Please arrive on time for each class. The session starts with structured introductions that set the tone for the hour.

Rescheduling

We need 24 hours' notice to reschedule a class. Less than 24 hours' notice counts as a cancellation of that class.

No-shows

A no-show counts as a cancellation of that class. If something comes up, please let us know as soon as possible.
