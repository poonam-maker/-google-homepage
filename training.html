Puppy Social Club

A 3-week program for puppies 8–16 weeks, led by a Certified Master Trainer. Help your puppy develop social confidence and the skills they'll rely on for the rest of their life — while you learn how to support them at home.

Program overview

What is Puppy Social Club?

A small-group program where puppies (8–16 weeks) meet other puppies in a safe, supervised setting and learn to "speak dog" — developing the social confidence and communication skills they'll need for the rest of their lives. Parents and puppies learn together.

Social skills

Learn to "speak dog"

Puppies are introduced to each other in a safe, supervised setting so they can learn to communicate and play — building social skills they'll rely on for the rest of their lives.

Foundation habits

Build a solid foundation

Learn how to prevent unwanted chewing, nipping, biting and jumping. Understand the importance of schedules, boundaries and limitations — the cornerstones of a confident, well-adjusted puppy.

For you too

Owners learn alongside

Discover your puppy's personality and how they communicate with other dogs. Learn when to intervene, when to redirect, and what motivates your puppy most.

What's covered

Each session includes

40 minutes of group observation, discussion, demonstration and hands-on learning, followed by 20 minutes of individual Q&A with our Certified Master Trainer.

Supervised puppy play

Structured introductions and monitored free play so your puppy builds confidence around other dogs in a safe, controlled setting.

Reading body language

Learn how puppies communicate — see your puppy's personality emerge and understand what they're telling you and other dogs.

When to step in

Understand when to correct, redirect or simply let play happen — and what motivates your puppy most.

Schedules, boundaries & limits

Learn why structure matters and how to set your puppy up with the habits that will shape their whole life.

Preventing problem behaviours

Proactive guidance on jumping, chewing, nipping, biting and fear — before these become ingrained habits.

Individual Q&A

20 minutes every session with our Certified Master Trainer — bring your specific questions and anything you're struggling with right now.

Is this right for my puppy?

What to expect

Who it's for

Puppies between 8 and 16 weeks who would benefit from structured socialization — and owners who want to build a solid foundation from day one.

What to bring

Your puppy on a leash, high-value treats they love, and your questions. Let us know any behaviour concerns at registration so your trainer can be prepared.

Policies

What to know before you join

Arrive on time

Clients are required to be on time for each session. The group dynamic and dog introductions are structured from the start — late arrivals disrupt the experience for everyone in the class.

Rescheduling and cancellations

24 hours' notice is required to reschedule a session. Requests made with less than 24 hours' notice will be treated as a cancellation of that session.

Any concerns about your puppy?

Let us know at registration. Jumping, chewing, nipping, nipping at other dogs — anything you'd like the trainer to know before the first class helps us prepare.
