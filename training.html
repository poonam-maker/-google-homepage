Doggy Social Club

Program overview

What is Doggy Social Club?

A focused 3-week program — 1 hour, twice per week — where dogs meet other dogs in a calm, structured environment while their owners learn the skills to support them at home.

Social skills

Learn to "speak dog"

Dogs are introduced to each other in a safe, supervised setting so they can learn how to communicate and play — skills they'll carry for the rest of their lives.

Foundation habits

Build a solid foundation

Learn how to prevent unwanted chewing, nipping, biting and jumping. Understand the importance of schedules, boundaries and limitations for a well-adjusted dog.

For you too

Owners learn alongside

Discover your dog's personality, how they communicate, and what motivates them most. Learn when to intervene and when to let them work it out themselves.

What's covered

Each session includes

40 minutes of group observation, discussion, demonstration and hands-on learning, followed by 20 minutes of individual Q&A with our Certified Master Trainer.

Supervised dog play

Structured introductions and monitored free play so your dog builds confidence around other dogs.

Reading body language

Learn how dogs communicate — see your dog's personality emerge and understand what they're telling you.

When to step in

Understand when to correct, redirect or simply let play happen — and what each situation calls for.

Motivation and reward

Identify what drives your dog most and learn how to use it effectively in training.

Preventing problem behaviours

Proactive guidance on jumping, chewing, nipping, fear and reactivity toward other dogs.

Individual Q&A

20 minutes every session with our Certified Master Trainer — bring your specific questions and current challenges.

Is this right for my dog?

What to expect

Good for dogs who…

Are 1 year or older, could benefit from structured socialization, have jumping, chewing, nipping or reactivity challenges, or whose owners want to understand them better.

What to bring

Your dog on a leash, high-value treats they love, and your questions. The trainer will note any behaviour concerns at registration so they can tailor the session.

Policies

What to know before you join

Arrive on time

Clients are required to be on time for each session. The group dynamic and dog introductions are structured from the start — late arrivals disrupt the experience for everyone in the class.

Rescheduling and cancellations

24 hours' notice is required to reschedule a session. Requests made with less than 24 hours' notice will be treated as a cancellation of that session.

Any behaviour concerns?

Let us know at registration. Jumping, chewing, nipping, fear, reactions to other dogs — anything you'd like the trainer to know before the first class helps us prepare.
