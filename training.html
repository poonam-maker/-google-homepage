Program overview

What is Puppy Social Club?

A focused 3-week program — 1 hour, twice per week — where puppies meet other puppies in a calm, structured environment while their owners learn the skills to support them at home.

Social skills

Learn to "speak dog"

Puppies are introduced to other puppies in a safe, supervised setting so they can learn how to communicate and play with other dogs — skills they'll use for the rest of their lives.

Foundation habits

Build a solid foundation

Learn how to prevent unwanted chewing, nipping, biting and jumping. Understand the importance of schedules, boundaries and limitations for a well-adjusted pup.

For you too

Parents learn alongside

Discover your puppy's personality, how they communicate, and what motivates them most. Learn when to intervene and when to let them work it out themselves.

What's covered

Each session includes

40 minutes of group observation, discussion, demonstration and hands-on learning, followed by 20 minutes of individual Q&A with our Certified Master Trainer.

Supervised puppy play

Structured introductions and monitored free play so your pup builds confidence around other dogs.

Reading body language

Learn how dogs communicate — see your puppy's personality emerge and understand what they're telling you.

When to step in

Understand when to correct, redirect or simply let play happen — and what each situation calls for.

Motivation and reward

Identify what drives your puppy most and learn how to use it effectively in training.

Preventing problem behaviours

Proactive guidance on nipping, chewing, jumping, biting and other common early challenges.

Individual Q&A

20 minutes every session with our Certified Master Trainer — bring your specific questions and current challenges.

What to know before you join

Arrive on time

Clients are required to be on time for each session. The group dynamic and puppy introductions are structured from the start, so late arrivals disrupt the experience for everyone.

Rescheduling and cancellations

24 hours' notice is required to reschedule a session. Requests made under 24 hours will be treated as a cancellation of that session.
